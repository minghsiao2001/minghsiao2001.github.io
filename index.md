## About Me
I am now a *Master 2 student* at [Université de Toulouse](https://fsi.utoulouse.fr/departement-mathematiques). 
## Research Interests
I am now enjoying differential geometry, geometric analysis, metric geometry, and complex geometry. My main research interests focus on geometric flows and their topological applications, especially Ricci flow. During my bachelor's degree, I also studied free probability theory and random matrix theory.
